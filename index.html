Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University

I am an MS Robotics student at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. David Held. Before joining CMU, I was an undergraduate researcher at UC Berkeley with Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka.

My research focuses on learning for manipulation, particularly in noisy and unfamiliar environments. I am working towards a future where robots can do anything humans can do, and more!
